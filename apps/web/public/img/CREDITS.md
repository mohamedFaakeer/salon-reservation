# Photography

Seven photographs from Unsplash, bundled rather than hot-linked so the app has
no third-party runtime dependency and works on a cold Render instance.

All are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free
for commercial use, no attribution required. The credits below are courtesy,
not obligation.

**These are placeholders.** The stylist portraits are stock models, not Ishara,
Kasun, Nadeesha or Tharushi. A salon going live should replace all seven with
their own photography; nothing in the code assumes these specific files beyond
the paths in `src/lib/imagery.ts`.
